Project Overview

The Challenge

Battery distributors operating across European markets face an increasingly complex regulatory landscape. The EU Battery Regulation mandates precise tracking and reporting of every battery unit placed on the market — yet most companies were managing this through fragmented spreadsheets, email threads, and manual PDF submissions prone to error and delay.

PowerLoop needed a centralized web platform that could replace this chaos: one place to submit placement data, generate compliance reports, track progress across regions, and give operations teams real confidence they were meeting regulatory obligations.

"The core challenge wasn't technical — it was clarity. How do you take dense regulatory data and turn it into something a non-technical operations manager can act on in seconds?"

Goals & Objectives

01
Replace manual spreadsheet workflows with a structured digital submission system for battery placement data.
02
Provide real-time compliance dashboards surfacing critical gaps and deadlines before they become regulatory violations.
03
Enable multi-country, multi-user access with role-based permissions for managers, admins, and auditors.
04
Generate audit-ready reports with a single action — reducing reporting time from days to minutes.
05
Build a scalable design system adaptable to EU regulation updates and future market expansions.
User Research

Understanding real needs

I conducted stakeholder interviews with 8 participants across operations, compliance, and management roles — mapping their processes end-to-end to identify every friction point in the current reporting workflow.

①
Operations teams spent 4–6 hours per reporting cycle manually cross-referencing shipment logs — with no version control and frequent data conflicts.
②
Compliance managers had zero real-time visibility into submission status across markets, discovering gaps only days before regulatory deadlines.
③
Reports were built manually in Excel → PDF → email, with no audit trail and high risk of version mismatch between stakeholders.
④
Users wanted instant clarity: how many batteries placed, in which markets, against which targets — without opening another tab or running a pivot table.

Design Process

From research to launch

01
Research & Discovery
Stakeholder interviews and workflow mapping sessions with operations and compliance teams. Mapped the full reporting cycle end-to-end — from shipment receipt to regulatory submission.
02
Design & Ideation
Low-fidelity wireframes for all four modules. Tested navigation structures and dashboard layouts. Explored single-page forms, step-by-step wizards, and CSV batch upload patterns before landing on a hybrid approach.
03
Prototyping
High-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma covering the full submission flow, dashboard drill-down interactions, and report generation. Tested simultaneously with compliance managers and ops staff.
04
Testing & Validation
Task-based usability testing on three critical flows: submitting a placement batch, identifying a compliance gap, and generating a quarterly report. Achieved sub-3-minute task completion by the final round.

Key Features

What we built

01
Compliance Dashboard
Real-time overview of battery placement status across all registered markets — totals, targets, gaps, and deadlines in a single scannable view. Drill-down per country and battery category.
02
Smart Batch Submissions
Guided forms or CSV batch upload with real-time validation. Flags anomalies before submission. Full audit trail with timestamps and user attribution for every data change.
03
One-Click Reports
Quarterly and annual compliance reports generated automatically — formatted to regulatory standards, downloadable as PDF or structured data. Days of work reduced to seconds.
