Project Overview

The Product

Vitaly is a mobile health application designed to empower individuals to track and manage their overall well-being through personalized data collection and insights. The app enables users to monitor key health metrics such as physical activity, sleep quality, and medical symptoms, offering a comprehensive digital diary for daily health tracking.

With intuitive visualizations and structured logging tools, Vitaly supports both everyday wellness and long-term health management.

The Problem

01
Fragmented tracking of health data across multiple apps or paper journals.
02
Lack of centralized access to personal health records and symptom history.
03
Difficulty identifying patterns or communicating health changes effectively to healthcare providers.
04
Limited awareness of holistic well-being due to isolated data points (steps, sleep, pain).

User Research

Understanding real needs

To ensure Vitaly meets real user needs, I conducted qualitative research with 10 participants, including individuals managing chronic conditions, fitness enthusiasts, and people interested in preventive health. The goal was to understand how people currently track their health, what challenges they face, and what they expect from a digital health companion.

①
Users often forget to log data due to complex or time-consuming interfaces.
②
Many rely on memory or paper notes, leading to inconsistent tracking.
③
There's a strong desire for simplicity, visual feedback, and long-term progress insights.
④
Users want to share their health history with doctors, but lack organized data.
Information Architecture

Structuring the experience

The app is structured around four core pillars of well-being: physical activity, sleep, medical symptoms, and a personal health journal. This architecture enables users to quickly access relevant data and visualize connections between different aspects of their health.

Information architecture diagram

Component Library

Design System

I designed a scalable component system using a calm, accessible color palette (WCAG 2.1 compliant). The library includes modular components for quick health logging, clear data visualization — progress rings, bar charts, stats cards — ensuring a seamless, trustworthy experience across all screens.

Design Process

From research to launch

01
Research & Discovery
Conducted qualitative interviews with 10 users to understand how they track their health and what pain points they experience. Key insights shaped the core structure — focusing on simplicity, quick logging, and clear visual feedback.
02
Design & Ideation
Created low-fidelity wireframes and tested multiple concepts to simplify health logging and data visualization. Focused on intuitive navigation, consistent iconography, and a clean hierarchy that supports daily tracking without friction.
03
Prototyping
Built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma. Tested core flows — dashboard overview, category logging, registry access — with real users to validate usability and refine interactions.
04
Testing & Validation
Ran usability tests to validate accessibility, flow efficiency, and daily tracking experience. Iterated based on feedback to ensure Vitaly feels effortless, trustworthy, and empowering.
