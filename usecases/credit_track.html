Overview

The Challenge

Users often feel overwhelmed or anxious when interacting with financial data, especially credit scores, which are abstract, hard to interpret, and tied to emotional stress. The challenge was to design an interface that demystifies credit behavior, translates complex data into actionable insights, and builds user confidence, not confusion.

Goals

  • Simplify loan management by making balances, due dates, and payment options instantly visible, turning financial complexity into clear, actionable steps.
  • Enable users to make advance payments or pay off debt in full with one tap, reducing stress and giving them control over their financial future.
  • Provide personalized, context-aware notifications for upcoming payments, rate changes, or document deadlines, helping users avoid penalties and stay compliant.
  • Build a secure, scalable design system that supports multiple loan types, currencies, and user roles (Personal/Business), ensuring consistency across all screens and future features.

Business Challenge

Balancing transparency with monetization: how to offer genuine value (free credit monitoring, personalized tips) while creating a sustainable business model (premium insights, partner offers), without making users feel “sold” or spammed.

User Research

To ensure CreditTrack meets real user needs, I conducted qualitative research with 10 participants, including individuals managing personal loans, small business owners with credit lines, and users recovering from late payments. The goal was to understand how people currently track their loans, what pain points they experience, and what they expect from a modern digital loan management tool.

I used semi-structured interviews to uncover behaviors, frustrations, and motivations around parking payments, ticket management, and notifications. Key questions included:

  • How do you currently track your loan payments and due dates?
  • What’s the most frustrating part of managing your loans?
  • Do you ever miss a payment? What usually causes it?
  • What would make managing your loans easier or less stressful for you?

Users often forget upcoming payments due to scattered reminders, unclear due dates, or lack of visual cues, leading to late fees and credit score damage.

Many rely on manual tracking methods like calendar notes or spreadsheets, resulting in inconsistent updates and missed deadlines.

There’s a strong desire for simplicity and instant clarity, users want to see at a glance: total debt, next payment amount, due date, and how to pay ahead, without digging through emails or statements.

Users want proactive, context-aware alerts, especially for rate changes, payment deadlines, or required documents, to avoid surprises and stay in control.

Information Architecture

The app is structured around four core pillars of loan management: Loan Overview, Payments & Actions, Profile & Security, and Documents & Insights. This architecture enables users to quickly access critical financial data, take action on payments, manage personal details, and retrieve important documents, all within a clear, intuitive navigation system.

Component Library

Created a scalable UI system with WCAG-compliant components, including loan summaries, payment buttons, and secure forms — to ensure consistency and fast implementation across all screens.
