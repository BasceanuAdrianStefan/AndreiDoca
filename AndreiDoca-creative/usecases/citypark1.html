Project Overview

The Challenge

Users faced a fragmented and stressful parking experience: unclear ticket durations, missed expiration alerts, complicated payment flows, and clunky interfaces that made managing parking feel like a chore. Existing solutions lacked real-time updates, intuitive navigation, and proactive communication—leading to fines, frustration, and app abandonment.

The Goals

  • Streamline the end-to-end process of purchasing, managing, and extending parking tickets.
  • Minimize cognitive load with a clean, consistent interface and one-tap actions.
  • Implement smart, timely notifications to warn users before tickets expire.
  • Enable personalized experiences—such as saved payment methods and license plates—for faster future use.
  • Establish a scalable design system that ensures visual and functional coherence across all screens.
